Modules

01

MODULE 1

Problems and algorithms

Formulate before you program.

About 12 hours

Outcome: you can state a computational problem precisely and trace an algorithm that solves it.

You will learn

  • Inputs, outputs, and state
  • Pseudocode and flowcharts

You will do

  • Trace a booking algorithm by hand
  • Design tests at and around a threshold

Completion check: you can explain why an exact-fit request fails a strict comparison.

What success looks like

Explain

You can say what a result means and what it cannot support.

Verify

You can choose tests that would reveal a wrong answer.

Weekly rhythm

Each week follows the same three moves, so you always know what comes next.

Launch

A short problem introduces the week.

Study

Readings and checks build the ideas.

Consolidate

A paper exercise brings them together.

  1. Read the problem and write down what you already know.
  2. Trace the worked example.
  3. Attempt the practice set before opening solutions.

Workload

Plan about 12 hours per module, spread over at least three days.
